Risks & guardrails

What must be managed before scale.

DATA QUALITY

Scores depend on current inputs.

Launch in a small geography, audit routes, refresh data and flag missing or stale context.

LIABILITY & TRUST

Never promise “safe.”

Use relative scores, clear explanations, privacy/legal review and emergency links.

TECHNICAL CAPACITY

API-backed MVP first.

Recruit developer capability before scale and avoid dependence on one data source.

LEGAL

Privacy by design.

A university deployment may involve location and other personal information; consent and privacy assessment matter.

SOCIAL

Trust must become habit.

Strong stated interest is not enough; repeated route choice is the real validation milestone.

COMPETITION

Big platforms can move fast.

The defensible edge is local deployment, institutional relationships, explainable context and Safe Stop partners.
